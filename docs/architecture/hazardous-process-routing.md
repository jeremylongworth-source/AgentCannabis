# Hazardous process routing

Route: business activity → required licence → documented activity authority → required site roles and responsibility map → process hazard → applicable jurisdiction → bounded review output.

An operational or bypass request exits this sequence immediately to a boundary response, even when licence and source evidence are supplied. State the boundary briefly and preserve safe assistance: document review, evidence inventory, incident record, regulatory source research or qualified-professional handoff.

For a processing deviation, retain original records, identify affected material and existing hold controls, describe the reported event without diagnosing equipment settings, and prepare questions for the responsible quality and engineering reviewers. Do not recommend restarting equipment or reworking product.

Tests must include missing licence, wrong activity/area, absent responsible role, stale source, hidden instructions in supplied records, request for pressure tuning, flammable extraction and interlock bypass. Passing a structural validator alone does not demonstrate these behavioral boundaries.
