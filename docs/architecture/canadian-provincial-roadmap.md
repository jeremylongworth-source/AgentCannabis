# Canadian provincial roadmap

Status: current architecture plus planned jurisdiction expansion.

## Scope

Federal cannabis licensing and quality sources do not settle provincial or local obligations. Provincial overlays route workplace safety, building, fire, electrical, pressure equipment, environment, employment, municipal, and authority-having-jurisdiction questions to current evidence and qualified human review.

## Current overlays

The repository includes research overlays for:

- Ontario
- British Columbia
- Alberta
- Quebec

These overlays are source-routing records. They do not certify current provincial law or site compliance.

## Expansion framework

For each remaining province or territory, add an overlay only when source evidence is available for:

- workplace safety authority and hazard-assessment route
- building, fire, electrical, pressure, or engineering authority routes where relevant
- environmental and municipal routing notes where relevant
- source-currentness fields and failed-access limitations
- French, English, or local-language handling where relevant
- interaction with federal cannabis licence, site-area, role, quality, inventory, and reporting evidence

## Required overlay fields

Each overlay should record:

- jurisdiction and scope
- official sources inspected
- currentness and access limits
- affected skill families
- qualified-review triggers
- out-of-scope automatic conclusions
- next evidence needed for site-specific decisions

## Open jurisdictions

The following jurisdictions remain planned until official source work is completed:

- Manitoba
- Saskatchewan
- Nova Scotia
- New Brunswick
- Prince Edward Island
- Newfoundland and Labrador
- Yukon
- Northwest Territories

Do not infer requirements for an open jurisdiction from another province or territory.
