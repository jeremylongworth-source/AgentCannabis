# Specialization roadmap

Status: v1.0 specialization boundary.

## Current state

AgentCannabis v1.0 defines 233 atomic skills and 18 professional skillsets. Professional skillsets are role wrappers that coordinate atomic member workflows through a self-contained member index. They do not create new legal, engineering, quality, tax, or regulated-filing authority.

## v1.0 professional skillsets

The v1.0 professional skillsets are the 18 wrappers in `skillsets/` and matching folders in `skills/`:

- cultivation-technician
- cultivation-manager-support
- master-grower-support
- plant-health-specialist
- controlled-environment-cultivation-specialist
- post-harvest-technician
- drying-curing-specialist
- post-harvest-manager
- cannabis-processing-technician
- processing-manager-support
- preventive-controls-specialist
- quality-control-specialist
- qap-support
- cannabis-quality-systems-specialist
- cannabis-compliance-specialist
- ctls-inventory-specialist
- cannabis-production-manager
- cannabis-operations-manager

## Expansion rule

Do not automatically add specialist roles beyond these 18. A new specialization requires:

- a concrete user or maintainer need
- affected taxonomy members
- safety and human-authority review
- source-currentness requirements
- install-path validation
- evaluation scenarios and actual behavior evidence
- documentation updates

## Deferred examples

The following may be considered later, but are not part of v1.0 readiness:

- jurisdiction-specific compliance coordinator roles
- tax/excise specialist roles
- engineering-document coordinator roles
- complaint or pharmacovigilance coordinator roles
- laboratory evidence coordinator roles

Each deferred role requires fresh acceptance criteria and evaluation before publication.
