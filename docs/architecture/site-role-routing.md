# Site role routing

Identify the holder, licence class, regulated role, named appointment, qualifications evidence where relevant, delegation/responsibility arrangement and decision being reviewed. Do not assume one person holds all roles or that the same roles apply to every licence class.

The taxonomy diagram's `identify-responsible-role` is an alias for `identify-required-site-roles` followed by `map-site-role-responsibilities`; it is not a 234th atomic skill.

| Decision context | Route for human review |
| --- | --- |
| Holder obligations, regulator contact and licence changes | Responsible Person / holder's authorized regulatory personnel, after checking applicable provisions |
| Cultivation oversight records | Master Grower responsibilities applicable to the licence |
| Quality oversight, batch approval and quality investigations | QAP responsibility and authorized human arrangements under the current Regulations |
| Security governance | Head of Security responsibilities applicable to the licence |
| Hazardous systems, building/fire/electrical questions | Qualified discipline professional and applicable authority having jurisdiction |
| CTLS and excise preparations | Separately authorized reporting owners for Health Canada and CRA |

The current Cannabis Regulations s.88(2) allows specified activities under QAP responsibility. Do not convert the human boundary into an inaccurate claim that only the QAP personally may perform every task. An AI assistant does not hold the regulated appointment and cannot sign a quality release or statutory submission. Verify the current section and actual site arrangements before stating who may decide.

Return a responsibility table: task, applicable requirement, proposed human owner, authority evidence, missing evidence, required review and record location. Unassigned responsibilities remain explicitly unresolved.
