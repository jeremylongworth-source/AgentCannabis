# Engineering boundaries

Prepare a handoff containing the activity description, jurisdiction, site/area, material identifiers, equipment identifiers, existing approved drawings/reports, documented hazards, discrepancies, affected lots/records, named owner and questions for a qualified professional. Omit configuration and operating instructions.

Escalate pressure-system configuration, flammable extraction, hazardous electrical environments, explosion protection, fire protection, critical ventilation and building/fire-code implications. Do not calculate equipment ratings, recommend bypasses, select protective devices or prescribe process settings. Do not translate a supplied hazardous SOP into an easier operating guide.

A professional's title or an asserted commercial licence does not establish that a specific design or process is approved. Preserve the distinction between identifying applicable authorities and certifying compliance. Provincial safety and construction requirements must be researched for the actual location and project scope.

Specialist expansion is governed by [the specialization framework](specialization-roadmap.md), not automatically enabled by this core repository.
