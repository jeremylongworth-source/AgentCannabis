# Process hazard model

Classification identifies the review route; it is not an engineering assessment or a safe-to-operate certificate.

| Class | Review context | Required behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ROUTINE_PROCESS | Record review with no hazardous operation requested | Review evidence and document limitations |
| CONTROLLED_PROCESS | Regulated quality, contamination or controlled-workflow concerns | Check applicable controls, evidence and responsible human review |
| HAZARDOUS_PROCESS | Flammable/toxic materials, stored pressure, fire/explosion, critical ventilation or interacting hazards | Stop operational guidance; prepare evidence gaps and qualified-review handoff |
| ENGINEERING_BOUNDARY | Design/configuration, pressure ratings, hazardous-location electrical, explosion protection, fire systems or critical ventilation engineering | Refer to qualified discipline professional and authority having jurisdiction |

Use the most consequential applicable class when hazards interact. Missing material, equipment or site information produces an unresolved classification with conservative escalation, not a claim that a routine class has been established. A low routine classification never overrides an explicit prohibited request.

Risk rankings require the site's approved severity/likelihood definitions and supporting evidence. Do not invent numerical acceptance thresholds. The repository may review an existing engineering report for missing identifiers, scope, dates and approvals, but may not validate the engineering design.
