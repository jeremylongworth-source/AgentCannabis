# AgentCannabis scope boundaries

Status: CC-01 repository scope. These are product boundaries, not a legal opinion about a site's activities.

## Included support

| Domain | Permitted artifact | Required limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Activity and licence authority | Activity classification, authority evidence checklist, questions for the licence holder | Do not infer authorization from an activity name, role title, or another site's licence. |
| Roles and governance | Responsibility matrix, access-governance review, proposed review assignments | Do not appoint regulated personnel or assert that the model occupies their role. |
| Cultivation and propagation | Record-completeness review, observation schema, provenance checks, deviation summary | No cannabis production schedules, growth targets, propagation protocols, or yield/potency optimization instructions. |
| Environment, root zone, and plant health | Measurement-quality review, comparison with supplied authorized criteria, evidence gaps, investigation questions | No operational setpoints, feeding/irrigation recipes, treatment instructions, or unverified diagnosis presented as fact. |
| Harvest and post-harvest | Lot identity checks, segregation-record review, chain-of-custody gaps, hold-status summary | No operational harvest, trimming, drying, or curing protocols. |
| Processing and refinement | Process classification, documented flow review, batch-record reconciliation, control-evidence gaps | No operating, configuring, tuning, or scaling cannabis extraction or manufacturing processes. |
| GPP and quality systems | Document-control review, administrative SOP structure, training-record checks, deviation/CAPA package | Drafting an SOP does not authorize an excluded procedure or make the document approved. |
| Testing and quality assurance | COA evidence review, sampling-integrity questions, comparison with verified specifications, release-readiness package | No invented results, omitted outliers, automatic compliance finding, or lot release. |
| Products and packaging | Classification research, specification/label review, draft notification package | No unsupported legal limits, product formulation recipe, marketing health claims, or submission. |
| Inventory, CTLS, and excise | Reconciliation, discrepancy explanation, lineage map, reporting-readiness package | Keep systems distinct; preserve source records; no filings or concealment. |
| Complaints and post-market control | Complaint triage, traceability analysis, proposed recall scope, draft reporting package | Authorized humans decide holds, recalls, reporting, and release from hold. |
| Workplace safety | Hazard-record classification, training-evidence review, escalation brief | No engineering design, interlock bypass, or replacement of qualified site review. |

## Jurisdiction and authority

Use the current federal source for federal cannabis questions. Add a provincial or territorial module only when the site and activity require it. Ontario, British Columbia, Alberta, and Quebec require independent source review; a rule found for one is not a default for another. Preserve authoritative French wording and identify translation uncertainty when reviewing Quebec sources.

Licence class, individual conditions, site areas, product category, activity, and relevant dates are separate inputs. A claim that a business is licensed is context; it does not establish all of these facts. Missing or conflicting evidence produces a limited review with explicit gaps.

Pest-control questions require current product authority and label applicability research before any compliance conclusion. The repository contains no static list of approved pesticide uses. Allowed work remains authority and record review, not instructions for applying treatment.

## Boundaries that require a different scope

Industrial hemp, advanced laboratory methods, cannabis research licensing, specialized provincial distribution, environmental compliance specialization, and advanced process validation are post-core candidates. Listing them in a roadmap does not implement or approve them.

Advanced extraction engineering, flammable-solvent systems, pressure systems, hazardous-location electrical work, and specialist fire/building design are outside the general repository. Provide an evidence-preserving escalation brief for a qualified professional; do not supply design or operating parameters.

## Handling an out-of-scope request

State the relevant boundary briefly, omit the excluded procedure, and preserve useful support such as record review, general regulatory research, a missing-evidence checklist, or a qualified-review brief. A request framed as licensed, hypothetical, urgent, or previously approved does not change the repository's content boundary.

If the scope of a request is unclear, ask only for facts needed to route a permitted review. Do not solicit unnecessary sensitive business information. Public fixtures use fictional sites, licences, personnel, results, and inventory, clearly labelled as test data.

## Verification

Positive cases must demonstrate useful bounded outputs. Negative cases must test missing licence evidence, wrong jurisdiction, missing role, stale sources, missing sampling evidence, operational recipe requests, hazardous-equipment requests, record manipulation, and quality-release overreach. Record actual outputs and reviewer findings separately from expected results.
