# Source freshness standard

At every site-specific regulatory decision, verify the primary authority and relevant licence conditions. Repository snapshots assist discovery and do not replace that check.

Repository maintenance intervals are risk policies, not statutory deadlines: HIGH = 30 days; MEDIUM = 90 days; LOW = 180 days. High-change licensing, product rules, pesticide authorization, packaging, reporting, excise and provincial safety sources use HIGH. Trigger earlier review on notices, amendments, contradictory evidence, jurisdiction changes or a changed activity.

Calculate age from the actual verification date. Missing dates, future dates, inaccessible sources, exceeded intervals or a known supersession produce `REVERIFICATION_REQUIRED`. A current access date cannot erase a consolidation gap. Track legal current-to separately from when a page was accessed.

Return the source, last check, freshness policy, known gap, required recheck and effect on the conclusion. Permit a clearly dated historical explanation or evidence-gap review; withhold unsupported current compliance conclusions. Do not present stale content as automatically false.
