# Output contract

Lead with the supported review conclusion and its limits. Include:

1. Task, jurisdiction, evidence period and review scope.
2. Known facts with source identifiers; assumptions and unknowns separately.
3. Licence/role/hazard route and source-currentness status.
4. Task-specific findings with criterion, evidence, gap, consequence, proposed review owner and next evidence needed.
5. Calculations with units, basis, formula, rounding and reconciliation where relevant.
6. Proposed follow-up, unresolved issues and the authorized human decision still required.

Use `REVIEW_SUPPORT_ONLY`, `MORE_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`, `OUTSIDE_DOCUMENTED_AUTHORITY`, `QUALIFIED_REVIEW_REQUIRED` or `BOUNDARY_REDIRECTION`. These are assistant workflow statuses, not statutory categories. `REVERIFICATION_REQUIRED` is a separate source status and does not mean the underlying rule is false.

Do not conclude a lot is releasable from one compliant COA. Separate sample representativeness, complete applicable testing, specifications, deviations, packaging, traceability and current hold state. Final approval is an authorized human act under the applicable responsibility arrangement.

Draft forms and plans retain a visible draft label and blank human signature fields. Never fabricate dates, evidence, approvals, licence numbers, analytical results, inventory adjustments or submission receipts.
