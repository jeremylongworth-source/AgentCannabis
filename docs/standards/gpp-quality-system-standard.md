# GPP and quality-system standard

Use the applicable current Cannabis Regulations Part 5 and relevant product/activity provisions. GPP scope and additional processing requirements must be checked by activity; a generic checklist is not a certificate of compliance. Source mapping is in [federal foundations](../research/federal-foundation-sources.md).

Every quality review preserves controlled document identity/version, effective date, owner, approval, training evidence, execution records and deviations. Distinguish proposed SOP text from an approved instruction. A change requires documented impact assessment, affected records/material, validation evidence where applicable, reviewer and implementation/training status before it is described as effective.

For sanitation, hygiene, cross-contamination, storage and building conditions, compare records with applicable requirements and site-approved controls; do not invent cleaning chemicals, concentrations, exposure settings or production parameters. For maintenance/calibration, trace equipment IDs, schedules, work records, calibration status and the impact of out-of-tolerance findings on affected evidence.

Supplier and ingredient/material qualification reviews separate supplier approval, incoming identity/specification evidence and product-specific admissibility. Prior supplier approval alone does not establish lot acceptance or ingredient legality.

Deviation/CAPA reviews identify containment evidence, affected lots/time window, facts versus hypotheses, root-cause evidence, actions, owners, due dates, effectiveness criteria and closure evidence. Do not erase nonconforming records or infer successful closure from an action being scheduled.

Quality release requires a complete evidence package and an authorized human decision under current QAP responsibility provisions. The assistant may identify gaps and organize a draft package; it cannot approve a batch or remove a hold.
