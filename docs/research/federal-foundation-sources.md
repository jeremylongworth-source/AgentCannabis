## Currency and authority

The retrieved [Cannabis Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-24.5/) states current to **2026-06-21**, last amended **2026-03-26**. The retrieved [Cannabis Regulations](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144/) state current to **2026-06-21**, last amended **2026-06-04**. Both display a page modification date of 2026-08-20.

These are three different dates: source access, consolidation coverage and page modification. None establishes the effective date of every provision. Amendments and commencement instruments after the consolidation coverage date were not exhaustively checked. Operational currency is therefore **REVERIFICATION_REQUIRED**. A later access date must not silently be substituted for the consolidation date.

The registry leaves original publication and provision-specific effective dates null where they were not independently established. Its proposed 30-day source-review interval is repository policy, not a legal rule; material decisions require fresh checks sooner when necessary.

## Human roles and quality approval

The responsible person has authority to bind the holder and is responsible for licensed activities. Head-of-security requirements have analytical-testing and research exceptions. [Regulations, ss.37-38](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144/page-6.html)

Micro/standard cultivation and nursery holders retain a master grower with responsibility for cultivation, propagation and harvesting. [Regulations, ss.12 and 15](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144/page-3.html)

Processing QAP requirements include qualifications and investigation responsibilities. Edible-cannabis expertise, alternate appointments and reference-standard/test-kit exceptions need separate checks. Current s.19(3) permits one or more qualified alternates; do not copy the older two-alternate limit. [Regulations, s.19](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144/section-19.html?wbdisable=true)

**Important approval nuance:** s.88(1) assigns method, preventive-control-plan and lot/batch approval duties; s.88(2) permits activities under paragraphs (1)(b)-(e) to be conducted under the QAP's responsibility. Do not claim that the QAP must personally perform every task. [Regulations, s.88](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144/section-88.html)

Repository policy should require evidence of the authorized human role and any applicable delegation. AI can organize findings and missing evidence; it must not impersonate a QAP or turn a readiness assessment into a release authorization. This is the repository's human-authority boundary, not a claim that legislation explicitly discusses AI.

## GPP and preventive-control findings

Applicable GPP is a precondition to sale, distribution and export. Section 80 requires SOPs for the stated production, handling and testing activities. Sections 79.1-79.3 contain scope exclusions, including analytical-testing and research distinctions. Sections 82-86 address quality during storage/distribution, premises and equipment. [Regulations, ss.79-86](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144/page-8.html)

Section 87 addresses sanitation. Section 88.1 specifically addresses competence for edible-cannabis activities. Section 88.93 requires applicable extract/edible producers to analyze biological, chemical and physical contamination hazards and substantiate control effectiveness. These contamination provisions do not provide engineering approval for hazardous plant or equipment. [Regulations, ss.87, 88.1 and 88.93](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144/page-9.html)

The written preventive-control-plan requirement applies to processing holders conducting activities relating to extracts or edible cannabis. Its required evidence covers hazards, controls, critical points/limits, monitoring, corrective action, verification and implementation. Do not generalize that specific requirement to every cannabis activity. [Regulations, s.88.94](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144/section-88.94.html)

Health Canada's guide explains GPP evidence and makes clear that testing alone is insufficient. Its displayed QAP discussion lacks the current s.88(2) nuance, so legislation governs that issue. The guide displays 2025-07-15 as its page date; this review does not validate every numerical example or testing detail in it. [GPP guide](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/cannabis-regulations-licensed-producers/good-production-practices-guide/guidance-document.html)

Document control, training records, calibration evidence, supplier review, deviation management, change control and CAPA can be repository quality practices. Any statement that a particular practice is legally mandatory must cite its applicable provision or condition separately; do not present the full proposed quality system as verbatim federal law.
