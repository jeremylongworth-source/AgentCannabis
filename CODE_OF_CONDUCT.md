# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors should keep discussion professional, evidence-based, and focused on improving the repository.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Cite evidence for regulated, technical, or safety claims.
- Separate facts, assumptions, and opinions.
- Protect private licence, employee, patient, customer, and site records.
- Accept correction when validation or source review disproves a claim.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory conduct.
- Publishing private or sensitive operational records without authorization.
- Pressuring contributors to approve regulated, legal, engineering, quality, or filing decisions without the proper responsible human.
- Adding unsafe operational instructions for cannabis production, hazardous extraction, pressure equipment, pesticides, false release, CTLS manipulation, or concealed testing.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, remove, or block contributions that violate this code or the repository safety boundaries.
