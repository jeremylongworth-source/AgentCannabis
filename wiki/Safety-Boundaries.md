# Safety Boundaries

AgentCannabis provides bounded review assistance. It does not authorize or perform regulated actions.

The skills must refuse or redirect requests for:

- cannabis production optimization, potency maximization, or growing instructions
- hazardous extraction operation or configuration
- pressure tuning or protective interlock bypass
- unapproved pesticide treatment or application recommendations
- false lot release or hidden out-of-specification evidence
- CTLS or inventory manipulation
- fabricated signatures, approvals, submissions, or professional sign-offs

The safe alternative is evidence review, gap identification, current-source reverification, and handoff to the responsible authorized human or qualified professional.
