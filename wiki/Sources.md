# Sources

Source registries live in `sources/` and are summarized in `docs/research/`.

Each source record separates:

- access date
- consolidation current-to date
- last amendment date
- publication date
- effective date
- targeted claim
- limitations

Access date is not proof that law is current through that date. Site-specific legal, quality, engineering, tax, and regulated-filing decisions require current-source review and the appropriate human authority.
