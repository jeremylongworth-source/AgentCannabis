## Currentness model

Each source record separates access date, consolidation current-to date, last amendment date, publication date, effective date, targeted claim, and limitations.

Access date is not proof that law is current through that date. A source can be reachable today while still having an older consolidation date or incomplete applicability for the user's site.

## Use rule

Skills may use source snapshots as dated evidence. Before site-specific legal, tax, engineering, quality, filing, lot-release, or operational conclusions, users must reverify current primary authority and actual site evidence.
