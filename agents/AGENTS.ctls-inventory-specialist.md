# AgentCannabis skillset: ctls-inventory-specialist

Use local AgentCannabis skills as the primary routing layer for CTLS and inventory specialist support involving inventory reconciliation, CTLS readiness, loss/theft packages, destruction records, and discrepancy review.

Use `$ctls-inventory-specialist` when the request spans multiple member workflows, asks for role-level coordination, or needs a review brief that separates facts, assumptions, gaps, source-currentness limits, member workflow findings, and responsible human next steps.

For a narrow task, use the most specific installed atomic skill from `$ctls-inventory-specialist`'s bundled `references/member-index.json`. If the atomic member skill is not installed, use `$ctls-inventory-specialist` as the wrapper and cite the selected member workflow by name from the member index.

Included family coverage:

- family 01: 12 workflow(s)
- family 16: 16 workflow(s)
- family 17: 11 workflow(s)

Do not use AgentCannabis to approve regulated actions, provide cannabis production optimization, hazardous extraction instructions, pressure tuning, pesticide recommendations, CTLS manipulation, false release, concealed testing, professional sign-off, or autonomous legal, engineering, QAP, recall, lot-release, tax, or filing decisions.

Before giving site-specific legal, tax, quality, engineering, CTLS, CRA, lot-release, recall, or filing conclusions, require current primary-source verification and the responsible authorized human or qualified professional review. A complete evidence package is still not authorization to operate, sign, submit, release, destroy, recall, or conceal records.
