# British Columbia cannabis overlay

Status: research overlay, not a site-specific compliance determination.

## Scope

This overlay records British Columbia evidence routes for AgentCannabis skills, especially high-pressure extraction hazards, chemical agents, pressure equipment, and workplace-safety escalation.

## Current evidence

The source registry includes:

- `BC-CANNABIS-PRESSURE-ADVISORY`: WorkSafeBC advisory on high-pressure cannabis extraction hazards.
- `BC-OHS-PART5`: WorkSafeBC Part 5 chemical and biological substances source location.
- `BC-OHS-PART12`: WorkSafeBC Part 12 tools, machinery, and equipment source location.

The registry does not establish every applicable permit, technical standard, Technical Safety BC requirement, municipal condition, or current consolidation status.

## Skill behavior

British Columbia requests should:

- classify reported pressure, flammable, ventilation, electrical, and fire/explosion issues as qualified-review candidates
- avoid equipment settings, extraction procedures, restart advice, or control-system changes
- preserve source-currentness limits and authority questions
- identify missing equipment IDs, professional documents, approval references, site scope, and responsible safety owner

## Release limits

This overlay supports evidence routing only. It does not certify safe operation, code compliance, pressure-equipment acceptance, or hazardous-process readiness.
